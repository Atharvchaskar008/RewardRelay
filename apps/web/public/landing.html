FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

The Inquiry

Answers to the practical questions teams ask before putting credit card into operation.

Answers to practical questions buyers and cardholders ask before putting escrow into operation.

Do I ever share my credit card?

No. The cardholder orders in their own verified account and ships to you. No card number, CVV, password, login credential or OTP ever changes hands at any point in the transaction.

What if an order is cancelled?

The payout stays frozen in escrow until delivery is verified. If the seller misses the deadline, the contract refunds you automatically.

What do I need to start trading?

Just a phone number. An embedded wallet is created for you. If you already use Web3, connect BridgeKey or MetaMask.

Why is SAKER on MST Blockchain?

Your funds sit in immutable open-source code that no middleman can touch, double-spending is impossible, and reputation travels with you.

Is SAKER currently live?

Yes. SAKER is live on MST Testnet with test MSTC tokens so you can safely simulate deals end-to-end with zero financial risk before mainnet deployment.
