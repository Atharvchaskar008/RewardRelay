Built for trust.
Designed for strangers.

SAKAR puts the money, the deadlines and the reputation on-chain, so two people who have never met can still make a deal safely.

Escrow on every deal

The Seeker's payment is held by a smart contract and released only when the benefit arrives.

Skin in the game

Both sides lock a deposit. Miss a deadline or cheat, and your deposit goes to the other side.

Fair disputes

Disputes are ruled by an arbiter. If nobody rules within 72 hours, the deal settles 50/50 automatically.

Two ways to share.
One way to trust.

Whether you buy for someone or pass a code along, the flow is the same: lock it, deliver it, release it.

Buy-for-you

You order with your own card offer, delivered to the Seeker's name and address. Funds release once the return window ends.

Pass-on

Your shareable code is sealed in a vault and revealed only to the Seeker who paid, followed by a 2-hour inspection window.

Trust Badge

Every settled deal earns a soulbound on-chain badge. Deal limits grow from ₹500 to ₹10,000 and deposits shrink.
